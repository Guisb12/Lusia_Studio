# Prompt for AI Image/SVG Generation

Create a clean, modern SVG illustration for a landing page section. The concept is "6 tools being replaced by 1 platform".

## Layout
- **Horizontal composition**: Left side → Center → Right side
- Clean, minimal style with flat design
- Professional SaaS/tech aesthetic
- Colors: use the brand colors provided

## Left Side: The Discard
- A modern trash/recycle bin icon (simple geometric shape, not too detailed)
- 6 app icons appearing to fall into or hover near the bin
- The 6 tools are:
  1. **Kahoot** (purple #864cbf) - simple "K" letter
  2. **PowerPoint** (orange #d24726) - simple "P" letter
  3. **Excel** (green #217346) - simple "X" letter
  4. **Word** (blue #2b579a) - simple "W" letter
  5. **Teams** (purple-blue #6264a7) - simple "T" letter
  6. **ChatGPT** (teal #10a37f) - simple hexagon or "C"

- Style for tool icons: simple rounded squares with white letters, slightly tilted/rotated (5-15 degrees) to suggest motion
- Subtle motion lines or arcs showing them being "thrown away"
- Small label below: "6 ferramentas. Múltiplas contas." in light gray

## Center: The Transition
- A simple arrow or transition element pointing right
- Could be a subtle curved path or just clean whitespace
- Optional: small text "substituído por"

## Right Side: The Solution
- **LUSIA Studio logo/placeholder** - a stylized "L" or geometric shape in #15316b (dark blue)
- Clean card/container shape
- Text: "LUSIA Studio" in bold
- Subtext: "Uma plataforma. Tudo integrado."
- Small feature pills below: "Calendário · Materiais · TPCs · Chat IA"

## Overall Style Requirements
- Flat design, no gradients (or very subtle)
- Clean lines, geometric shapes
- White or light gray background
- Professional, trustworthy feel (this is for educators/schools)
- Aspect ratio: wide banner format (3:1 or 4:1)
- SVG format, scalable, minimal code

## Mood
- Liberating (getting rid of clutter)
- Simple, clean, organized
- Modern but professional

## Reference Style
- Linear.app illustration style
- Notion landing page graphics
- Clean vector art, no photorealism
